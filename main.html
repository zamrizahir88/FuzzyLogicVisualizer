About FuzzyLogic Visualizer

Version: 2.0

Author: Dr. Zamri, Universiti Malaysia Perlis (UniMAP)

Objective: A professional academic platform for designing, visualizing, and exporting Fuzzy Inference Systems (FIS).


Supported Inference: Mamdani & Sugeno

Supported MFs: Triangular, Trapezoidal, Gaussian, Bell, Sigmoidal, Singleton

Export Formats: PNG, SVG, PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON, MATLAB (.m/.fis), Scilab (.sce), Python (.py), C++/Arduino.
