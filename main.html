💡 Fuzzy Logic Examples

🟢 1. Smart Fan Controller Easy

Maps room temperature directly to fan speed. Single input, single output — perfect for beginners.

🧪 10°C → Fan 18% 🧪 25°C → Fan 50% 🧪 40°C → Fan 82%
🟢 2. Plant Watering System Easy

Checks soil moisture to determine how many seconds a water pump should run.

🧪 Dry → Pump 16s 🧪 Moist → Pump 10s 🧪 Wet → Pump 1s
🟡 3. Tip Calculator Medium

Combines Food + Service quality using OR/AND logic to determine a tip percentage.

🧪 Bad+Bad → Tip 7% 🧪 OK+Great → Tip 15% 🧪 Great+Great → Tip 22%
🟡 4. Streetlight Dimmer Medium

Saves energy by keeping lights dim unless it is dark AND motion is detected.

🧪 Bright → Off 🧪 Dark+NoMotion → Dim 🧪 Dark+Motion → Bright
🔴 5. Auto Braking System Hard

Complex 3-input system (Speed, Distance, Road Wetness) to decide braking force.

🧪 Far+Slow → Brake 0% 🧪 Close+Moderate → 60% 🧪 Close+Fast+Wet → 89%
